Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about DriveNow car rental services

❓

How do I book a car?

Visit our Fleet page, choose your vehicle, fill the booking form and confirm. You'll receive a confirmation call within 1 hour.

💳

What payment methods?

We accept cash, debit/credit card, and bank transfer. Payment is required at the time of pickup.

🔄

What is the cancellation policy?

Free cancellation up to 24 hours before pickup. Late cancellations may incur a Rs. 500 fee.

📋

Documents required?

A valid CNIC and driving license are required. International tourists need a valid passport and international driving permit.
